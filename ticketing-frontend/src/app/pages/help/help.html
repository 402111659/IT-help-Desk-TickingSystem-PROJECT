PASSWORD & ACCOUNT

Forgot your password?

If you cannot access your account, reset your password using the available password recovery option.

SOLUTION

Reset your password and try logging in again. If you still cannot access your account, create a support ticket for IT assistance.

02
📶
WI-FI & INTERNET

Wi-Fi isn't connecting?

Check your Wi-Fi connection and make sure your device is connected to the correct network.

SOLUTION

Turn Wi-Fi off and on again, reconnect to the network and restart your device. If the issue continues, contact IT Support.

03
🖨️
PRINTER PROBLEMS

Printer isn't working?

Check whether the printer is powered on and connected to the correct network or computer.

SOLUTION

Check the printer connection, paper and error messages. Restart the printer and try printing again.

04
💻
COMPUTER PROBLEMS

Computer is slow?

Slow performance can be caused by too many applications running at the same time.

SOLUTION

Close unnecessary applications and restart your computer. If the problem continues, submit a support ticket.

STILL NEED ASSISTANCE?

Can't find a solution?

Submit a support ticket and an IT technician will assist you with your problem.
